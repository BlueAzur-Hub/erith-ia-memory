From 70666 to a new machine

More than a modification.

Rising Phoenix began with the idea behind LEGO NINJAGO set 70666, The Golden Dragon. The mechanical dragon is the heart of the project: a longer creature with a stronger frame, visible energy details and a modular architecture.

Over four months, the physical model evolved through rebuilding, targeted part purchases and mechanical refinement. The finished physical dragon was then reconstructed in BrickLink Studio so its structure and modular logic could become a complete instruction guide.

The public project deliberately stops at three official build levels. A later personal dual-reactor evolution exists outside the published instructions and inventory.

Designed, built and documented by Christophe78.
