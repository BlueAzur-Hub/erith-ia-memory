Origin · LEGO NINJAGO 70666

From one set to a complete creation.

Rising Phoenix began from the idea and parts language of LEGO NINJAGO set 70666, Master Wu's Golden Dragon. The goal was not to catalogue versions: it was to build a stronger, longer, more mechanical dragon and to preserve the whole path that led to it.

The physical MOC came first. It was dismantled, rebuilt, extended, tested and refined with real bricks. New parts were acquired when the architecture demanded them. The final machine was then reconstructed digitally so the real build could become a reproducible instruction guide.

This page tells that story image by image: origin, dismantling, parts, rebuilding, three official configurations, engineering, Temple story, Studio reconstruction, instructions and the final project economics.

Designed, built and documented by Christophe78.
