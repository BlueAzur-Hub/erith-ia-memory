From 70666 to a new machine

More than a modification.

Rising Phoenix began with the idea behind LEGO NINJAGO set 70666, The Golden Dragon. The original silhouette immediately suggested something larger: a mechanical creature with a stronger frame, a visible energy system and enough space to carry a small structure on its back.

Over four months, the physical model evolved through repeated rebuilding, targeted part purchases and mechanical refinement. The result is a long, articulated display dragon that keeps the gold, dark green, black, dark gray and translucent green language of the source while developing its own architecture.

Designed, built and documented by Christophe78.

One dragon · three presentations

Choose how far the machine evolves.

The project was designed around removable and add-on modules, allowing three main display configurations.

01

Base Model

The mechanical core: articulated body, long segmented neck, four extended arms and a strong gold / dark-green visual identity.

02

Master Wu's Temple

A vertical architectural module adds the gong, lantern details and pink blossoms that give the dragon its calm Japanese-inspired ceremonial counterpoint.

03

Extended Rising Phoenix

The complete display configuration adds the developed propulsion section and expanded rear silhouette for the most dramatic final form.

Mechanical anatomy

Built to look engineered.

The design uses the decorative gold shell to frame a denser mechanical language underneath: gray structure, black pivots, dark-green bodywork and translucent green energy details.

Modular connection

Advanced propulsion

The rear assembly is treated as a distinct mechanical system rather than simple decoration. The connection, energy rings and translucent-green elements visually separate propulsion from the dragon's armored body.

Control center

Cockpit and central frame

The pilot sits inside the visual center of the model, surrounded by the dark-green structural ring and translucent energy accents.

Long-axis design

Silhouette first

From above, the dragon reads as one long mechanical line from head to propulsion, with the arms and rear structures creating a broad, balanced span around it.

Engineered to be real

Not only a Studio model.

Rising Phoenix exists as a complete physical build. The final shape was refined with real bricks, real weight, real articulation and real display constraints before the digital model and instructions were completed.

A quiet structure on a machine

Master Wu's Temple

The temple module creates the vertical signature of Rising Phoenix. Its gold pillars, central emblem, ceremonial accessories and small pink blossoms contrast with the long mechanical body beneath it.

The purpose is not realism. It is visual storytelling: a peaceful architectural landmark carried by a powerful mechanical creature.

Four months of creation

The model was discovered by rebuilding it.

The final design did not appear in a single session. It grew through physical experiments, dismantling, targeted parts and repeated changes of proportion.

Premium building guide

Rebuilt digitally, step by step.

After the physical model was finalized, the dragon was reconstructed in BrickLink Studio so that the complete project could be shared as a real building experience rather than only as photographs.
