The project

The dragon first.

Rising Phoenix began with LEGO NINJAGO set 70666 and became a long, articulated mechanical dragon through repeated physical rebuilding, parts searches and structural refinement.

The real MOC came first. BrickLink Studio came later to reconstruct the finished architecture, check its geometry and turn it into a reproducible building guide.

The published project stops at three official configurations. Each level is a valid destination; the later personal experiments are not part of this presentation.
