THE ORIGINAL SPARK

Set 70666 was the starting point — not the destination.

The public photo story begins with the earliest surviving rebuild photographs. What follows is not a catalogue of models: it is the actual transformation of one weekend hobby project into Rising Phoenix.

Parts are removed, searched, replaced and tested. Proportions change. The body becomes longer. The rear architecture evolves. Only after the physical MOC works does the digital reconstruction begin.

THREE PUBLISHED CONFIGURATIONS

One dragon, three valid endpoints.

Each level is complete on its own. The Temple and rear propulsion are modular additions, not proof that the lower levels are unfinished.

LEVEL 01

Dragon Speeder

The complete base mechanical dragon. No Japanese-style Temple and no Advanced Rear Propulsion Engine.

Indicative build budget · €50–60
LEVEL 02

Master Wu's Temple

The base dragon receives the removable Temple display module with Master Wu's golden Japanese hat, weapons and blossom details.

Indicative build budget · around €80
LEVEL 03 · PUBLISHED TOP LEVEL

Rising Phoenix

The Temple configuration gains Lloyd's Advanced Rear Propulsion Engine: the most developed configuration included in the published instructions.

Indicative build budget · €90–100
