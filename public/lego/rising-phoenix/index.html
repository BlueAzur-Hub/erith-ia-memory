From 70666 to a new machine

More than a modification.

Rising Phoenix began with the idea behind LEGO NINJAGO set 70666, The Golden Dragon. The mechanical dragon itself is the heart of the project: a larger creature with a stronger frame, a visible energy system and a modular architecture that can carry additional display elements.

Over four months, the physical model evolved through repeated rebuilding, targeted part purchases and mechanical refinement. The result is a long, articulated display dragon that keeps the gold, dark green, black, dark gray and translucent green language of the source while developing its own architecture.

The published project deliberately stops at three official levels. That keeps the build modular and gives each builder a choice: begin with the more accessible Dragon Speeder, add Master Wu’s Temple, or continue to the full rear propulsion configuration.

The finished physical model was then rebuilt digitally in BrickLink Studio so its structure, modular logic and construction sequence could be documented as a complete instruction guide.

Designed, built and documented by Christophe78.

Creator's personal evolution · not included in the instructions

Premium building guide

Rebuilt digitally, step by step.

After the physical model was finalized, the dragon was reconstructed in BrickLink Studio. That digital reconstruction became the working basis for organizing the 354 construction steps preserved in the 150-page PDF guide. The mechanical dragon is the core of the product, presented through three official levels: Dragon Speeder, Japanese Temple display, and Advanced Rear Propulsion. The accessories support the presentation rather than replacing the dragon as the main build. Later personal evolutions, including the second reactor, are intentionally left outside the instructions.
