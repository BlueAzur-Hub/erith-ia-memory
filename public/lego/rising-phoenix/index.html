ORIGIN · SET 70666

The box was only the starting point.

This page is not an encyclopedia of versions. It is the photographic story of a real MOC becoming something else: experiments, dismantling, parts searches, structural changes, three valid published configurations, digital reconstruction and finally instructions.

No replacement box image is fabricated here: the story begins with the earliest surviving physical photographs supplied for the project.
